## Reports

### Average podcast episode length in 2026
📄 [Full report](https://www.castfox.net/research/average-podcast-episode-length-2026?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=research) · 📊 [`data/average-podcast-episode-length-2026.csv`](data/average-podcast-episode-length-2026.csv)

- The typical episode runs **35 minutes** (median across 58,886 shows; 95% CI 34.8–35.3). The mean is 43 minutes because long-form shows pull it up.
- 43% of shows stay under 30 minutes. 21% run 60+ minutes, and 8% run 90+.
- Longest genres: TV & Film (59 min), Music (58), Leisure (53), Comedy (51). Shortest: Kids & Family (24), Education (25), News (27).
- English-language shows run 37 min. Italian (18) and French (22) shows are the shortest of the major languages measured.

### How often podcasts publish in 2026
📄 [Full report](https://www.castfox.net/research/how-often-podcasts-publish-2026?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=research) · 📊 [`data/how-often-podcasts-publish-2026.csv`](data/how-often-podcasts-publish-2026.csv)

- Weekly is the standard. 41.5% of active podcasts publish about once a week, and the median gap between episodes is 7 days.
- 10.4% of active shows publish daily, led by News (29.1% daily). 15% publish about monthly.
- Only 11.1% of all podcasts released an episode in 2026, and 6.8% did in the 90 days to September 30.
- Most podcasts fade early. 22.8% stopped after one episode, 52.8% never passed six, and only 6.9% reached 100.

### Podcast guest statistics 2026
📄 [Full report](https://www.castfox.net/research/podcast-guest-statistics-2026?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=research) · 📊 [`data/podcast-guest-statistics-2026.csv`](data/podcast-guest-statistics-2026.csv)

- 41% of English-language episodes feature at least one guest.
- Highest guest rates: Science (62.6%), Business (59.8%), Arts (57.5%). Lowest: Fiction (10.3%), Religion & Spirituality (25.8%), Comedy (33.1%).
- Episodes with guests average 1.28 guests, and 17% have two or more.
- Most-booked guests of 2026 so far, outside news and politics shows: Eric Ries (70 shows), Nir Eyal (58), Cynthia Thurlow (48).

### AI on podcasts 2026
📄 [Full report](https://www.castfox.net/research/ai-on-podcasts-2026?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=research) · 📊 [`data/ai-on-podcasts-2026.csv`](data/ai-on-podcasts-2026.csv)

- 4.8% of English-language episodes (January–September 2026) have AI as a topic, about 1 in 21.
- AI comes up most in Technology (48%), Business (17.1%) and News (7%), and least in Sports (0.1%), TV & Film (0.3%) and Religion & Spirituality (0.4%).
- AI companies mentioned most: OpenAI (3,219 shows), Anthropic (2,605), Nvidia (1,104), Google (997).
- "AI agents" is still a niche topic: about 1 in 600 episodes.

### Top podcast sponsors 2026
📄 [Full report](https://www.castfox.net/research/top-podcast-sponsors-2026?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=research) · 📊 [`data/top-podcast-sponsors-2026.csv`](data/top-podcast-sponsors-2026.csv)

- Quince ran ads with 327 podcast publishers (459 shows), more than any other brand, ahead of Shopify (287) and BetterHelp (264).
- Health & wellness (13 brands) and retail & home (12) make up about half of the top 50.
- Betting brands (PrizePicks, Kalshi, DraftKings) cluster in sports. PrizePicks is the most widespread sponsor on sports podcasts.
- Podcast advertising has a long tail: 26,623 distinct sponsor names, but only 374 advertised with 10+ publishers.

## About the data

- **Source:** the CastFox podcast index, built from public RSS feeds and episode content. Each report's page explains its sample, filters, and limits in a Methodology section.
- **Format:** some CSVs hold several tables one after another, each starting with a title line. These are the same tables as in the published report.
- **Aggregates only:** the files contain summary statistics, not raw episode records.
- **Snapshot:** 2026 figures. New reports are added monthly.
